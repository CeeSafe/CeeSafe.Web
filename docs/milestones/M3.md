# M3 — Streaming and SSE: record

Implementation plan §7 (M3). Toolchain: ceesafec **0.9.2** (`docs/toolchain.md`). Design: Web ADR 0008.

**Status (2026-11-02):** the exit gate passes: `web_sse` and `Exchange.peerClosed` are on main, with their sweeps,
and `examples/sse` passes the 60 s slow-client gate and the disconnect gate over TCP. Waiting upstream:
`Overflow.DropOldest` and batched flushes (CeeSafe U13). Release v0.1 (M0–M3) follows M2's 24-hour fuzz run.

## Design decisions

| Decision | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| M3 tasks 1–2 (body reader and writer, chunked, upload limit, min rate) are M2's `Exchange` | built in M2 for the HTTP/1.1 driver | Web ADR 0008 §1 |
| Disconnect is noticed by racing a read while idle (`Exchange.peerClosed`) | a second reader task would need the transport lent mutably twice; a cancelled read loses nothing, a cancelled write poisons | Web ADR 0008 §2, §3.1 |
| `EventQueue`: a bounded channel with `Block`, `Disconnect`, `DropOldest` | the plan's overflow policies | Web ADR 0008 §3.3 |
| `serveEvents` races the stream against an `EventSource`, which never wins | a producer only told through the queue ran a whole tick after the client left | Web ADR 0008 §3.4 |
| `DropOldest` refused, one flush per event, until U13 | `Channel` has no receive that does not wait | Web ADR 0008 §3.3, upstream U13 |

## Runs (2026-11-02, macOS arm64, ceesafec 0.9.2)

| Command | Result |
|---|---|
| `(cd packages/web_sse && ceesafec test)` | 7 passed: encoding vectors, overflow policies, byte-exact streams (events then the end; heartbeats under a quiet queue), a closed client freeing the producer, a sleeping producer cancelled, `Last-Event-ID` |
| `python3 tests/interop/sse.py --slow-seconds 15` | every check passes. Three events with ids 1–3, heartbeats between them, the body ended; resumption at 42 after `Last-Event-ID: 41`; a client that leaves an endless stream frees it and its producer in 1 ms (heartbeat 100 ms); at 1 KiB/s RSS went 4720 → 4224 KiB, 673 KiB produced against 15 KiB read (the rest in socket buffers); closing the slow client frees the flood in 1 ms; drain, exit 0 |
| `python3 tests/interop/sse.py` (60 s) | every check passes. RSS at 1 KiB/s over 60 s, sampled every 5 s: 4688 KiB at 5 s, 1952 KiB at 55 s (never above the first); 562 KiB produced against 60 KiB read; a closed client frees its stream and producer in 2 ms |
| `python3 tools/run_tests.py --sanitize --alloc-sweep --cancel-sweep` | 24 runs passed (exit 0). `web_sse`: 7 tests, 126 allocation-failure and 76 cancellation runs; the other packages' counts unchanged |
| the same, first attempt | `web_sse`'s allocation sweep timed out at allocations 4–7 of `a_closed_client_frees_the_producer`: a test whose stream task failed before starting left its producer waiting on a queue nobody closed. Fixed in the test (it closes the queue, as the stream does) |
| `tests/probes/sse-race-exchange.cee` | prints 3, 2, closed, interpreted and native: a race may borrow the exchange mutably in one arm |

## Exit gate (plan §7, M3)

| Gate | Status |
|---|---|
| A slow client (1 KB/s) against a producer at 100 MB/s keeps RSS flat | ✔ 60 s (`tests/interop/sse.py`) |
| A client disconnect frees the producer task and buffers within 1 heartbeat | ✔ 1 ms, unit test and interop |
| Cancel sweep over the SSE tests | ✔ 76 cancellation runs, 0 failures (plus 126 allocation-failure runs) |
