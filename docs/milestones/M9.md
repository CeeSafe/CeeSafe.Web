Implementation plan §7 (M9). Design: Web ADR 0013, accepted 2026-11-06 with the owner's six answers. Toolchain:
ceesafec **0.11.0** for stages 1 and 2; stage 3 needs **0.19.1** (`File.readAt`, `length`, CeeSafe ADR 0075), and

**Status (2026-11-06):** in progress, on branch `m9-io`. Stage 1 (profile) is done: the baseline and the profile are
below. Stage 2 is in progress: findings 1 and 3 are fixed (`21dc3af`), and finding 2 (`f0862fc`, which makes 0.19.1

## Findings

1. **Byte-at-a-time copies.** `OwnedBuffer.append` (`packages/web_core/src/buffer.cee:89`) copies with a loop of
   single-byte stores, and is the top function streaming 1 MiB over HTTP/1.1 (44 % of samples) and HTTP/2. CeeSafe has
   had `SliceMut.copyFrom` since 0.1.0 (type system §16.5): one length check and one `memcpy`.
2. **Byte-at-a-time zeroing.** `OwnedBuffer.create` (`buffer.cee:16`) fills its vector with one `push(0)` per byte. Each
   HTTP/2 stream creates a 16 KiB write buffer and a 4 KiB response head (`packages/web_http2/src/serve.cee:832`,
   `:836`), about 20 000 pushes a request; that is the top function for h2c `/small`. `Vector.resize(n, 0)` (CeeSafe
   0.15.0, ADR 0071) does it at once; a pool (stage 2) avoids it altogether.
3. **`Exchange.remaining()` over HTTP/2 is null** even when the request declares `content-length`
   (`packages/web_http/src/exchange.cee:388`: a stream body is `BodyState.Stream`). The benchmark's `/echo` then makes
   a 1 MiB buffer per request (`benchmarks/http/src/main.cee:64`), which explains h2c `/echo` at 2 k req/s against
   43 k over HTTP/1.1. The request's head carries the declared length (RFC 9113 §8.1.1), so `remaining()` can report it.
4. **HTTP/2 allocates per request** (38 for h2c `/small`, 0 for HTTP/1.1): the stream's task, channels and buffers. A
   1 MiB `/stream` costs 867 (h2c), 1 452 (TLS) allocations: one or more per DATA frame.
5. **TLS `/stream`**: 775 allocations per 1 MiB over HTTP/1.1, OpenSSL's (clear-text HTTP/1.1 makes none).

Findings 1–3 are fixes to make before stage 2's measurements: until they are made, they hide what pooling and vectored
writes would change.

### The data path's copy loops (2026-11-06)

A second profile (`sample`, h2c at 10 connections, after `f0862fc`): `/small` is now in the kernel (`sendto` 644,
`read` 466, `kevent` 407 samples, our code below 200), and `/stream` in two functions of our own with no callee,
`web_http2.handle` 815 and `StreamPort.write` 800: per-byte `push` loops moving every body byte at least twice per DATA
frame. The same shape was in the engine, the frame encoder and HPACK, web_http's field encoding and body reads, and
web_tls's transport. They now use `Vector.extend` (CeeSafe ADR 0071, 0.15.0) and `copyFrom`:
`web_http2/src/{serve,connection,frame,hpack}.cee`, `web_http/src/{fields,exchange}.cee`,
`web_tls/src/transport.cee`. `python3 tools/run_tests.py --ceesafec ceesafec-0.19.1 --sanitize --alloc-sweep
--cancel-sweep`: 43 runs passed, web_tls's interpreter run skipped. The client, proxy, WebSocket and setup code keep theirs for now (off the server's path).
